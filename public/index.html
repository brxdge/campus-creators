Before you ask

Still unsure about something? Send it over and we'll answer directly.

Ask us
How many creators come with a campaign?

It depends on the campuses you're targeting and the volume of content you need. We'll size it on the first call and quote against that rather than a fixed package.

Do we own the content afterwards?

Yes. You get the raw files and usage rights for both paid and organic. We agree the exact terms in writing before anything is shot.

How long does a campaign take?

Most campaigns run a few weeks from brief to delivered content. Live activations and events need more lead time, especially around exam periods and reading week.

Which campuses can you reach?

We work with over 1,000 students across Canadian campuses. Tell us the schools or regions you care about and we'll confirm our coverage there before you commit.

Can we start small?

Yes. Plenty of brands start with content only, see how it performs, then add ambassadors or a full activation for the next launch.
