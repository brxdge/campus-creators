Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Campus Creators ("we", "us") is a student creator and campus marketing company based in Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you fill in an ambassador or brand form or contact us, we collect what you provide, such as your name, email address, school, social media handles and your message.
  • TikTok account information (only if you connect your TikTok). If you choose to log in with TikTok, we receive your TikTok display name, profile avatar and a unique TikTok account identifier (open ID), and nothing else. We use TikTok's Login Kit and request only the user.info.basic permission.
  • Basic technical data. Our servers may log standard information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

How we use your information

  • To review ambassador and brand submissions and reply to you.
  • To confirm that a TikTok account belongs to the creator who connected it, and to link it to that creator's record in our internal roster.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website.

What we do not do with TikTok data

  • We do not post, comment or send messages on your behalf.
  • We do not access your videos, followers, direct messages or any other TikTok data beyond your display name, avatar and account identifier.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that host or operate our website for us, and when the law requires it. If you are an ambassador, we may show your name and public profile details to brands you are being considered for, as part of the service you asked us to provide.

Retention

We keep your information for as long as it is needed for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete it. TikTok access tokens are used only to confirm your connection and are not kept after they expire.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us at info@brxdge.ca.
  • You can disconnect Campus Creators from your TikTok account at any time in your TikTok app settings, or ask us to disconnect it and delete the related data.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@brxdge.ca.
