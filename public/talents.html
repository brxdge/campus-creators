Questions

The stuff people ask before applying. Still unsure? Just apply, it's free and there's no commitment.

Apply now →
Do I need a huge following?

No. We care more about how you make content and who's actually watching than a follower count. Micro-creators book campaigns here all the time.

Does it cost anything?

Never. Applying and being on the roster is free. You get paid by the brand for campaigns, so money only flows to you.

What kind of content?

Short-form mostly, the stuff you'd post on TikTok and Reels. Product moments, day-in-the-life, campus content, unboxings. Real, not overproduced.

Do I have to be a student?

Our network is built around campuses, so most creators are current students, but if you're campus-adjacent and make great content, still apply and tell us your situation.

How much do campaigns pay?

It varies by brand, deliverables and your reach. Every brief comes with the rate up front, so you decide before you commit. No surprises.

Ready to get on the roster?

Two minutes to apply. Free, no commitment, and you could be on your first campaign in weeks.
